# Signing in

<a id="realms-choices"></a>
## Choose your programme

**Where:** `/realms`.

Pick the programme you work in, such as Undergraduate or ICE, to go to its sign-in page. Programmes marked "soon" aren't open yet.

<a id="realms-download"></a>
### Just need a timetable?

You don't need an account to download one. See [Download a timetable](download.md).

<a id="login-form"></a>
## Sign in

**Where:** `/login`.

Enter the email and password for your UniSchedule account. The page shows which programme you're signing in to. Super Admins, Super Viewers and Super Administrators (CITS) can switch programme later, from the top bar.

<a id="login-realm"></a>
### Wrong programme?

"Choose another" goes back to the programme list.

<a id="login-forgot"></a>
### Forgot your password?

Goes to [Reset your password](#forgot-form).

<a id="register-form"></a>
## Accept an invitation

**Where:** the link in your invitation email (`/register`).

Choose a password and confirm it to finish setting up your account. If the link has expired or been used, ask whoever invited you to send a new one.

<a id="forgot-form"></a>
## Reset your password

**Where:** `/forgot-password`.

1. Enter your email. A code is sent to it.
2. Enter the code. **Use a different email** goes back to step 1.
3. Choose a new password.
