# CBT timetable

**Where:** CBT Timetable in the menu (`/timetable/cbt`). **Who:** Super Administrator (CITS).

The timetable for exams taken on computers (CBT). It works like the [exam timetable](timetable-exams.md), but lists only the courses marked as CBT. The grid is explained in [The timetable grid](timetable-grid.md).

<a id="cbt-manage"></a>
## Manage CBT courses

Choose which courses are taken as CBT exams. Search for courses, tick or untick them, and they appear on or disappear from this timetable. CBT status can also be set on a course on [Courses](courses.md).

The other controls work as on the exam timetable:

- [Draft or FINAL](timetable-lectures.md#tt-status)
- [Choose a semester](timetable-lectures.md#tt-semester)
- [Export](timetable-lectures.md#tt-export)
