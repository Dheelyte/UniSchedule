# Signatures & Stamps

**Where:** Signatures & Stamps in the menu (`/signoffs`). **Who:** Super Admin.

What's printed on every page of a FINAL timetable PDF: the stamp, the signatory's signature, name and title, and a QR code to check the copy. Drafts get a DRAFT watermark instead. Changes apply to the next export; PDFs already downloaded keep what they were printed with.

<a id="sign-intro"></a>
<a id="sign-card"></a>
## One for each timetable

The lecture and exam timetables each have their own card: their own signatory, signature and stamp.

<a id="sign-copy"></a>
## Copy across

Copies this card's details to the other timetable, so both are signed the same way.

<a id="sign-fields"></a>
## Signatory

The **name** and **title** printed under the signature, for example "Registrar". The title is required.

<a id="sign-images"></a>
## Signature and stamp

Upload a PNG or JPEG of each. For the best result:

- **Signature:** sign in dark ink on plain white paper, then scan or photograph it straight on.
- **Stamp:** stamp on plain white paper. A round or square stamp prints best.

**Remove** clears an image.

<a id="sign-clean"></a>
### Clean up the scan

Removes the paper background so only the ink prints.

<a id="sign-preview"></a>
## Preview

How the signature strip will look on the PDF.

<a id="sign-save"></a>
## Save

Saves the card. **Discard changes** goes back to what was saved.
