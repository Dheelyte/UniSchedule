# Change Requests

**Where:** Change Requests in the menu (`/requests`). **Who:** Super Admins review; Super Viewers can look.

Requests to change a course's slot, sent with [Request a change](timetable-lectures.md#tt-request-change) on a timetable page. Approving one applies the change straight away.

<a id="req-status"></a>
## Filter by status

Show pending, approved or rejected requests, or all.

<a id="req-table"></a>
## Requests

Each request: who sent it, the course, the kind of change, when, and its status.

<a id="req-view"></a>
## Review a request

**View** opens the request: the current slot, the change asked for, and the reason. A Super Admin can add a note and then:

- **Approve & Apply:** makes the change in the timetable. It's checked like any other edit, so a change that would land on blocked time or a booked shared hall is refused.
- **Reject:** leaves the timetable as it is.

The person who sent it is notified either way, with your note. Every decision is recorded in the [Activity Logs](activity.md).
