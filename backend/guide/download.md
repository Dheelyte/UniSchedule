# Download a timetable

**Where:** `/download`. **Who:** everyone; no account needed.

Download the FINAL timetables: those locked, signed and stamped by the timetable office. Drafts are never offered here.

<a id="dl-when"></a>
## Session and semester

Choose the academic session and semester. Only those with a FINAL timetable are listed. If a programme other than Undergraduate has FINAL timetables, you also choose the programme.

<a id="dl-type"></a>
## Lectures, exams or CBT

Choose which timetable you want. Only the kinds that are FINAL for that semester can be chosen.

<a id="dl-narrow"></a>
## Narrow it down

Optionally choose your faculty, department and level to get just your part. Only options with something in the timetable are listed.

<a id="dl-button"></a>
## Download

Downloads the file. Its pages carry the signatory's signature and stamp, and a QR code to check it later.

<a id="dl-verify"></a>
## Got a printed copy?

Check a printed timetable is genuine and still current. See [Check a printed copy](verify.md).
