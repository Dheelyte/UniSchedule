# Staff

**Where:** Staff in the menu (`/staff`). **Who:** Super Admins, and Faculty Editors for their own faculty. A Faculty Viewer who has handed out or received editor access sees just that part.

Who has access to UniSchedule in this programme, and what they can do. See [Roles](roles.md).

<a id="staff-invite" data-roles="SUPER_ADMIN, FACULTY_EDITOR"></a>
## Invite staff

Send an invitation by email. Choose:

- the person's **email address**;
- their **role**;
- their **faculty**, for faculty roles. A Faculty Editor's invitations are always for their own faculty;
- optionally, the **semester** to restrict them to.

They receive a link to [set their password](signing-in.md#register-form). The link expires; an expired one can be revoked and sent again.

<a id="staff-received" data-roles="FACULTY_EDITOR"></a>
## Editor access you hold

Shown when a Faculty Editor has given you their access. You can do everything a Faculty Editor can, but only they can take it back or pass it on. See [Editor access handovers](roles.md#editor-access).

<a id="staff-your-access" data-roles="FACULTY_VIEWER"></a>
## Your editor access

Shown to a Faculty Editor who has handed their access over. **Take it back**, or **give it to another Faculty Viewer** in your faculty.

<a id="staff-invitations" data-roles="SUPER_ADMIN, FACULTY_EDITOR"></a>
## Pending invitations

Invitations not yet accepted, with their role, faculty and expiry. **Revoke** cancels the link.

<a id="staff-accounts" data-roles="SUPER_ADMIN, FACULTY_EDITOR"></a>
## Active access lists

Everyone with an account: email, role, programme, faculty ("Global" for roles not tied to one) and when they joined.

<a id="staff-edit-role" data-roles="SUPER_ADMIN"></a>
### Edit role

Super Admin. Change someone's role, faculty or semester. It applies the next time they use UniSchedule.

<a id="staff-grant" data-roles="FACULTY_EDITOR"></a>
### Grant editor access

Faculty Editors, next to a Faculty Viewer in their faculty. Hands your editor access to them: they become the Faculty Editor and you become a Faculty Viewer until you take it back. HODs can't receive it.

<a id="staff-remove" data-roles="SUPER_ADMIN, FACULTY_EDITOR"></a>
### Remove access

Deletes the person's account after you confirm. They can no longer sign in. Their past activity stays in the log.

<a id="staff-handovers" data-roles="SUPER_ADMIN"></a>
## Active editor handovers

Super Admin. Every handover in effect: the faculty, who gave it, who holds it and since when. **Reverse** ends one and swaps the roles back.
