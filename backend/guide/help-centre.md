# Help centre

**Where:** Help centre in the menu, or `/help`. **Who:** everyone signed in.

This guide, inside UniSchedule. It only shows what your role can use: pages, sections and answers for other roles aren't shown to you.

Students and visitors have their own short list of questions at **FAQ** (`/faq`), linked from the public pages' header and footer.

<a id="help-search"></a>
## Search

Type a word, such as "lock", "conflict" or "export", to find every section that mentions it. Sections whose title matches come first. Click a result to go straight to it.

<a id="help-nav"></a>
## Every page

The guide's pages you have access to, down the side, grouped as in the app. The Help centre's home page lists them with a line about each. On a phone, use **← Help centre** at the top of a page to get back to the list.

<a id="help-content"></a>
## A page

Each feature has its own section. On longer pages, **On this page** at the top jumps straight to a section.

## Getting here from the app

- **Learn more in the guide** on every [page preview](page-previews.md) card opens the section about that feature, in a new tab, so the preview stays where it was.
- **Open the guide for this page**, in the [? menu](page-previews.md#tour-help), opens the page about where you are.

Every section has its own address (for example `/help/timetable-lectures#tt-export`), so you can share a link straight to it. Someone whose role doesn't include that section will see the rest of the page instead.
